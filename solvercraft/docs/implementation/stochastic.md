## Resource and numerical policy

Public DTOs reject unknown fields. Each expansion counts resulting variables, rows and terms, validates coefficient handles/domains and estimates conservative storage before cloning model data. Existing structural caps remain 10,000 variables, 50,000 rows and 1,000,000 terms. Additional bounded-work caps apply to covariance assembly, candidate comparison, information-value enumeration and Benders dual assembly/cut batches. Allocation failures return structured errors.

Weights are finite, nonnegative and have positive mass; they need not sum to one. Power-of-two scaling supports equal weights at `f64::MAX` and at the smallest positive subnormal. Unrepresentable relative weight ranges and severely ill-conditioned weighted arithmetic are rejected rather than silently dropping positive mass. Original objectives and original logical/risk constraints are checked after transformations. These checks use documented floating-point tolerances, not exact arithmetic.

Indicator expansion keeps the original RHS separate from M. Each signed inequality becomes `a*x-z<=rhs`, `0<=z<=M`, followed by `z+M*b<=M` (active at one) or `z-M*b<=0` (active at zero). Outward product/addition bounds derive M; every auxiliary variable, row and term is counted before allocation. This avoids losing a small active RHS when `M+rhs` is not representable.

Box auxiliaries now represent **weighted support**, `u_i>=radius_i*x_i` and `u_i>=-radius_i*x_i`, with coefficient one in the uncertain row. The earlier unweighted absolute-value form amplified its feasibility tolerance by the radius: fixed `x=1e-9`, radius `1e12` and uncertain RHS zero returned Feasible/Optimal despite original support `1000.0000000000001`. That unchanged regression now passes. Independent original validators also prevent a sum of individually small auxiliary-row violations from exceeding the original row tolerance. Budget support orders exact positive dyadic products before selecting full/fractional mass, then accumulates original products/triples with directed residual bounds; rounded-product ties and collective underflow cannot silently erase support. Checked results contain `{counterpart,result}`; result values retain the counterpart auxiliaries, while the reported objective/max violation are checked in original coordinates. A changed objective evaluation drops transferred bounds/Optimal status; an invalid original robust point is a numerical error rather than a feasible solution.
